## Humerus masks

Foreground voxels at full resolution: median 150,835, range 31,821-522,185. Ultrasound images the bone *surface*, not its interior, so these are shells rather than solid bones.

The masks come in **two MetaImage dialects**: ImFusion and Slicer. The ImFusion-written ones store a `Position` and `Orientation` that describe where the label map sat in the reconstructed scene, not the geometry of its own voxel grid -- for PA001_trial_A V12 the header `Orientation` equals that volume's reference rotation to 5e-7. Honouring those fields would apply the reference transform a second time. `read_label_map()` therefore ignores them and relies on index correspondence with the paired volume, which is verified two ways: identical `DimSize` and `ElementSpacing`, and the physical check that masked voxels are 1.82x-8.53x brighter than the volume mean (median 3.99x), which is what a bone surface looks like in ultrasound.

## Transforms

- 321 reference transforms parsed; all are rigid (unit scale, zero shear, orthonormal rotation), which the dataset's own workspace script also asserts.
- Robot poses parse and index-align with them: the relative rotation between consecutive volumes correlates at **r = 0.823**. Their relative *translations* do not reconcile, so the experiment does not use them (see `EXPERIMENT_DEVIATIONS.md`).
